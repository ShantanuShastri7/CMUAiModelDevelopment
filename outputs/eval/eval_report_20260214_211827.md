### Query 4: What is 'Self-RAG' and how does it work?
**Latency:** 1.91s
**Answer:**
SELF-RAG is a retrieval-augmented generation approach that enables a model to generate text with reflection tokens, allowing it to evaluate and improve its own output. The process works as follows: (1) the model determines if augmenting the continued generation with retrieved passages would be helpful and outputs a retrieval token to call a retriever model on demand, (2) it concurrently processes multiple retrieved passages, evaluating their relevance and generating corresponding task outputs, and (3) it generates critique tokens to criticize its own output and choose the best one in terms of factuality and overall quality (Source: 2310.11511v1, Chunk: 2310.11511v1_chunk_4). SELF-RAG also enables a customizable decoding algorithm to satisfy hard or soft constraints defined by reflection token predictions, allowing for flexible adjustment of retrieval frequency and customization of models' behaviors to user preferences (Source: 2310.11511v1, Chunk: 2310.11511v1_chunk_9).
